How to Use This Page
Pick a category tab below, then click one or more factor pills. Selecting one factor sorts players by that real number directly. Selecting multiple factors (even across different category tabs) combines them into a Composite Match score from 0-100 - it's the average of how well each player ranks against everyone else on every factor you've picked. 100 means best-in-the-field on everything selected; 0 means worst. Click any player's row to see their real game log, filterable by pitcher, day, and opponent.
Model
HR Probability - the single best all-around estimate of how likely this batter is to homer today. Blends everything below into one number.
Power - overall raw ability, based on exit velocity, barrel rate, and home run production.
Matchup (opp. pitcher HR/9) - how home-run-prone today's opposing pitcher has been. Lower number = tougher pitcher to face.
Recent Form - home runs over their last 15 games. A hot-streak indicator, not a guarantee it continues.
Platoon Edge - how well they hit same-handed vs. opposite-handed pitching, applied to today's actual starter.
Vs Today's Mix - how well this batter specifically handles the exact blend of pitch types (fastball, slider, etc.) today's pitcher actually throws.
Power Metrics
Barrel % - share of batted balls hit with the ideal exit velocity + launch angle combo. The single best predictor of raw power.
Exit Velocity - average speed the ball leaves the bat. Higher = harder contact.
Hard-Hit % - share of batted balls hit 95+ mph.
Season vs. L15 - full-year rate vs. just their last 15 games. Comparing the two shows whether their underlying contact quality is trending up or down, not just their luck.
ISO - extra-base power per at-bat, a cleaner power read than slugging.
SLG / AVG / OBP - standard total bases per at-bat, batting average, and on-base rate.
Matchup
Pitcher HR/9 Percentile - how today's starter ranks among ALL of today's starters for homers allowed. Higher percentile = juicier matchup.
Bullpen ERA / WHIP - quality of the opposing relief corps, relevant if the batter sees the bullpen later in the game.
RISP AVG - batting average with runners in scoring position.
Park & Weather
Park Factor - how much a park inflates or suppresses home runs vs. a neutral park (1.00 = neutral, higher = hitter-friendly).
Wind Score - how much today's wind should help (positive) or hurt (negative) fly balls.
Temperature - warmer air is thinner; fly balls carry slightly farther.
Team Context
Projected Total HR - a real, math-derived estimate of total home runs for the game or team, built by adding up every individual starter's own real HR estimate - not a separate guess.
Lineup Spot Bonus - reflects batting order. Hitters higher in the order get more at-bats, and therefore more chances.
Situational
Day of Week / Home vs Road / Day vs Night - real historical splits. Always check the PA count next to these - a hot Tuesday rate over 12 PA is noise, not signal.
Vs. Pitch Type
Real career performance against every pitch type today's specific opposing pitcher actually throws, weighted by how often he throws it. A hitter who mashes sliders facing a pitcher who throws 40% sliders is a real, specific edge the main board doesn't spell out directly.
Season Vs. Pitch Type
Same real data, but unfiltered - shows performance against a pitch type across the WHOLE season, regardless of today's opponent. Use this to answer "who's the best fastball hitter in baseball," not just "who's good today."
Making Good Picks
  • Don't chase one number alone. A hot Recent Form with a bad Matchup is closer to a coin flip than a real edge.
  • Watch the ⚠ warning icon - it means under 15 PA for that specific number. It's a real stat, just a noisy one.
  • Combining 2-4 related factors (e.g. Power + Matchup + Vs Today's Mix) into a Composite Match tends to be more reliable than staring at any single number.
  • This page exists to show you why a player ranks where he does on the main board, or to surface an angle - like a specific pitch-type edge - the main board doesn't spell out on its own.
